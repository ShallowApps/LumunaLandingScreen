Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Lumuna is made by Shallow Apps. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Lumuna app and this website. In short: Lumuna works entirely on your device, and we don't collect your data.

Camera

Lumuna uses your front camera to show makeup on your face in real time. Face tracking and makeup rendering happen on your device. Camera images are never uploaded, stored by us, or shared with anyone.

Photos

Photos you take are kept inside the app on your device. If you choose to save a photo, Lumuna asks for permission to add it to your photo library. Lumuna only adds photos; it does not read your existing library.

What we don't collect

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No personal information, face data, or images sent to us or to third parties.

Settings

Your preferences, such as timer sound or default intensity, are stored on your device only.

Deleting your data

You can delete all photos from Settings in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Copies you saved to Photos stay there until you remove them.

Children

Lumuna doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@shallowapps.com.
