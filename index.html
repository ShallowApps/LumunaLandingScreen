Common questions

When can I get Lumuna?

Lumuna is coming soon to the App Store.

Is this real makeup or a cosmetic product simulator?

No. Lumuna is a playful lifestyle app, like a little beauty toy for your camera. The makeup is a digital effect for fun and self-expression. It does not apply real cosmetics or predict how a particular product will look on your skin.

How do I try a shade or change its intensity?

Choose a makeup category and tap a shade. Adjust its intensity to soften or build the effect. Choose None in that category to remove it. Settings → Default intensities controls how strong newly applied makeup starts.

How do I save a look for later?

Take a photo with makeup applied, then choose Save look from the photo’s menu and give it a name. Your saved look keeps the makeup recipe so you can use it again.

Can I share a photo?

Open a photo and use the share action, or save a copy to your photo library. Nothing is shared automatically; you choose the destination.

The mirror is black or asks for the camera

Lumuna needs the camera to show makeup on your face. Open the Settings app, go to Privacy & Security → Camera, and turn on Lumuna.

I can't save photos to my library

Open the Settings app, go to Privacy & Security → Photos → Lumuna, and allow adding photos.

The makeup doesn't line up with my face

Face the camera in good, even light and keep your whole face in view. Turning on the face guide in Lumuna's Settings helps you line up.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Lumuna does not upload your camera feed or kept photos to our servers. You can choose to export or share a photo; your device’s backup and photo-sync settings may also apply. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my photos?

In Lumuna, open Settings and tap Delete all photos, or delete them one by one in your library. Saved looks are managed separately. Copies exported to Photos or shared elsewhere stay there until you remove them.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Lumuna is a virtual makeup mirror for iPhone. It uses your camera to show makeup on your face, live. We do not collect any personal data through the app, and everything it does with your camera happens on your device. If that ever changes, we will update this page.

Face tracking happens on your device.No account. No analytics. No ads. No tracking.

Camera

With your permission, Lumuna uses the front or rear camera to show a live mirror. It does not use the microphone. You can turn camera access off at any time in your device’s Settings.

Face data

To place makeup on your face, Lumuna finds points on your face in live camera frames, such as around your eyes, lips, and face outline. It uses these points only to line up the makeup, show where a product goes, and tell whether a face is in view. This runs on your device, using the MediaPipe Face Mesh model included in the app. Lumuna does not use face data to identify you.

The camera feed and face points are kept only in memory while the app is running. The most recent face points stay in memory until new ones replace them or the app closes. Lumuna does not record the camera feed, does not save face points, and does not send either to anyone.

Photos

When you take a photo, Lumuna saves it in the app’s private storage on your device, with the date and the makeup you were wearing. If makeup was on, Lumuna may also save a copy of the photo without makeup. These photos may show your face. They stay in the app until you delete them or delete Lumuna.

Lumuna itself does not upload your photos. If you save a photo to Apple Photos, Lumuna uses add-only access to your photo library, and that copy may sync through iCloud Photos if you use it. If you share a photo, it goes to the app you choose. If you back up your device, the backup may include your in-app photos.

A saved look keeps only its name, date, and makeup settings. It has no photo and no face data.

Settings

Your preferences, such as timer sound and default makeup intensity, are stored on your device. Lumuna also keeps a count of photos you saved to Apple Photos from the photo review screen, only to decide when to ask for an App Store rating. Lumuna has no cloud storage or sync. Device backups may include these settings along with Lumuna’s other files.

Deleting your data

Tap Retake to delete the photo you just took. You can delete photos one at a time in Lumuna’s library, or all at once with Delete all photos in Lumuna’s Settings. These controls apply to both copies of a photo, with and without makeup. Saved looks are deleted separately. Deleting Lumuna removes everything it stored. Copies you saved to Apple Photos, shared, or backed up are not removed by Lumuna.

If you choose to contact support

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you.

Children

Lumuna doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies. If you choose a light or dark appearance, that preference is saved in your browser’s local storage. Choosing System clears the saved preference. Fonts and preview images are served with the site. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.
